# Review a short emotional scene

A practical viewing checklist for a two-character reunion story. These are editorial review criteria, not extra controls in the website or promises about every generated frame.

## First viewing: can you follow the decision?

- Can you identify the survivor and returning companion?
- Does the survivor's expression make recognition and hesitation understandable?
- Is the change from fear to affection visible without a written explanation?
- Does the embrace resolve the scene rather than interrupt it?

## Second viewing: look at continuity

- Check faces across cuts, especially eyes, mouth and hair.
- Check that hands, arms and contact points remain plausible during the embrace.
- Watch for distracting changes in clothing, background or body position.
- Listen for an abrupt music ending or an unexpected silent section.
- Check the downloaded file in the format you intend to share.

If something fails, describe the timestamp and the visible problem: for example, “at 00:09 the survivor's left hand changes shape.” A specific observation is more actionable than “the video looks wrong.”
